# Mountain Goats — Standard Mode Rules

## Overview

Mountain Goats is a multiplayer dice board game for **2–10 players**. Race your goats up six mountains, collect point tokens from the summits, and earn bonus tokens by completing full sets. The player with the most points wins!

---

## Setup

### Mountains

There are **six mountains** numbered **5 through 10**. Each mountain has a different height (number of steps to reach the summit):

| Mountain | Height (steps) | Point Tokens |
|----------|---------------|--------------|
| 5        | 4             | 12           |
| 6        | 4             | 11           |
| 7        | 3             | 10           |
| 8        | 3             | 9            |
| 9        | 2             | 8            |
| 10       | 2             | 7            |

> **Player scaling:** For **2–3 players**, fewer tokens are available: remove `4 - numPlayers` tokens from each full stack. For **4–10 players**, use the full 4-player token stacks unchanged.

### Goats

Every player starts with **one goat at the foot of each mountain** (six goats total).

### Bonus Tokens

Four bonus tokens are available, worth **15, 12, 9, and 6 points** respectively.

---

## Turn Structure

### 1. Roll Dice

On your turn, roll **4 dice** (standard six-sided).

**The Ones Rule:** If you roll **two or more 1s**, keep one and **re-face the rest** to any value you choose (2–6). This gives you more control over your groupings.

### 2. Group Dice

Assign your dice into **groups**. Each group's sum must equal a mountain number (**5–10**). A group moves that mountain's goat **up one step**.

- You may use **any number of dice** in a group (1, 2, 3, or all 4).
- Each die can only be used **once**.
- You don't have to use all your dice — unused dice are simply wasted.
- You can form **multiple groups** in one turn to climb multiple mountains.

**Examples:**
- Dice: `3, 2, 4, 1` → Group `3+2` = Mountain 5, Group `4+1` = Mountain 5 (climb twice!) or `3+4` = Mountain 7, `2+1` = skip.
- Dice: `5, 5, 3, 2` → Group `5` = Mountain 5, Group `5` = Mountain 5, Group `3+2` = Mountain 5 (three climbs on Mountain 5!).

### 3. Climb

For each valid group, your goat moves **one step up** the corresponding mountain.

### 4. End Turn

After climbing (or choosing not to), your turn ends and play passes to the next player clockwise.

---

## Summits & Scoring

### Reaching the Top

When your goat reaches the **summit** (top) of a mountain, you **take a point token** from that mountain.

- Each point token is worth the value of its mountain: **5, 6, 7, 8, 9, or 10 points**.
- This applies to **all player counts (2–10)**.

### Single Occupancy

Only **one goat** can occupy the summit at a time. If you reach a summit where another player's goat already stands, their goat is **bumped back to the base** of that mountain.

### Harvesting

If your goat is **already on the summit** and you climb that mountain again (matching group), you **harvest** — take another point token without moving. Your goat stays on top.

### Empty Mountains

When a mountain runs out of point tokens, goats can still climb and bump, but no tokens are awarded.

---

## Bonus Tokens

Collect at least **one token from each of the six mountains** to complete a **full set**. When you do, you claim the next available bonus token:

| Bonus | Points |
|-------|--------|
| 1st   | 15     |
| 2nd   | 12     |
| 3rd   | 9      |
| 4th   | 6      |

You can earn **multiple bonuses** — each additional full set (minimum count across all six mountains increases) claims the next token.

---

## Endgame

The game enters a **final round** when either:

1. **All four bonus tokens** have been claimed, OR
2. **Three or more mountains** are emptied of point tokens.

Once triggered, every player gets **equal turns** (the round completes so no one has fewer turns than anyone else). Then the game ends.

---

## Winning

The player with the **highest total score** wins. Your score = point tokens collected + bonus token points.

### Tie-breaking

Ties are broken in order:
1. **Most summit positions** (goats currently on top of mountains)
2. **Highest summit value** among those positions

### Winner Slots

The number of winners depends on the table size:

| Players | Winners |
|---------|---------|
| 2–4     | 1st place only |
| 5–7     | Top 2 |
| 8–10    | Top 3 |

---

## Quick Reference

- **Dice:** 4 per turn
- **Mountains:** 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10
- **Climb:** Group dice to sum to a mountain number → move up 1 step
- **Ones Rule:** 2+ ones → keep 1, re-face the rest
- **Summit:** Take a token, bump the occupant
- **Token value:** Always equal to the mountain value (5–10)
- **Harvest:** Already on top + matching group → take another token
- **Bonus:** Collect from all 6 mountains → claim bonus (15/12/9/6)
- **End:** All bonuses claimed OR 3+ mountains empty → equal turns → highest score wins
- **Winner slots:** 2–4 players → 1 winner; 5–7 → top 2; 8–10 → top 3
