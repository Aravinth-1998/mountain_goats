No ranked players yet.

  1. Six mountains 5–10. You have a 🐐 at the foot of each. Heights: 5,6→4; 7,8→3; 9,10→2.
  2. On your turn, roll 4 dice (multiple 1s: keep one, re‑face the rest).
  3. Group dice — each group whose sum is 5–10 moves that mountain's goat up one space.
  4. Reach a top to take a Point Token (and bump the goat there off). Already on top? A match harvests another token.
  5. Collect from all 6 mountains (a set) to claim a Bonus Token ✨ (15/12/9/6).
  6. End when all Bonus Tokens are gone or 3 mountains empty, then equal turns. Most points wins. 🏆
  1. Teams of 2 or 3 players share a combined score. Supported: 4p→2 teams, 6p→2 or 3 teams.
  2. Teammates can share summits — no bumping between teammates.
  3. Each player collects their own tokens, even if a teammate is already on that summit.
  4. If an opposing player reaches a team-held summit, all teammate goats there are bumped back to base (Team Wipeout).
  5. Turns alternate between teams (Red→Blue→Red→Blue…).
  6. Team with the highest combined score wins. Same tie‑break rules apply at team level.
