    How to play

    1. Six mountains 5–10. You have a 🐐 at the foot of each. Heights: 5,6→4; 7,8→3; 9,10→2.
    2. On your turn, roll 4 dice (multiple 1s: keep one, re‑face the rest).
    3. Group dice — each group whose sum is 5–10 moves that mountain's goat up one space.
    4. Reach a top to take a Point Token (and bump the goat there off). Already on top? A match harvests another token.
    5. Collect from all 6 mountains (a set) to claim a Bonus Token ✨ (15/12/9/6).
    6. End when all Bonus Tokens are gone or 3 mountains empty, then equal turns. Most points wins. 🏆
