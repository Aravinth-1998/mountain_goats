# Mountain Goats Team Play Variant (v1.0)

## Overview

This variant introduces team-based gameplay while preserving all original Mountain Goats movement, dice, mountain, scoring, and end-game rules.

Supported team configurations:

- 4 Players → 2 Teams of 2
- 6 Players → 3 Teams of 2
- 6 Players → 2 Teams of 3

All standard rules apply unless explicitly modified below.

---

## Team Setup

### Team Assignment

Divide players into teams.

Examples:

### Teams of 2
- Team Red: Player A + Player B
- Team Blue: Player C + Player D

### Teams of 3
- Team Red: Player A + Player B + Player C
- Team Blue: Player D + Player E + Player F

Each player continues to control their own goats.

---

## Shared Team Score

Each team uses a single shared score track.

Whenever a player would score points, those points are added to the team's score.

All scoring rules from the base game remain unchanged.

---

## Team Summit Occupancy

Teammates may occupy the same summit space.

Examples:

### Team of 2
Mountain 5 Summit:
- Player A goat
- Player B goat

### Team of 3
Mountain 5 Summit:
- Player A goat
- Player B goat
- Player C goat

All teammates may occupy the summit simultaneously.

---

## Summit Scoring

A summit controlled by a team scores only once, regardless of how many teammate goats occupy that summit.

Example:

Mountain 5 Summit:
- Player A goat
- Player B goat

Result:
- Team scores for Mountain 5 once.

Additional teammate goats on the summit do not generate additional points.

---

## Team Summit Conquest

If a player reaches a summit occupied by an opposing team:

1. All opposing-team goats occupying that summit are immediately removed.
2. Removed goats are returned to the base of that mountain.
3. The attacking goat takes control of the summit.

### Example

Before:
- Team Red has two goats on the summit of Mountain 7.

Blue Player reaches the summit.

After:
- Both Red goats are returned to the base of Mountain 7.
- Blue Player's goat occupies the summit.

This is referred to as a Team Wipeout.

---

## Strategic Options

Teams may choose to:

### Stack
Place multiple teammates on the same summit for stronger positional control.

### Spread
Control multiple summits simultaneously using different team members.

Both strategies are valid and supported by the rules.

---

## Team Variant Summary

1. Players are divided into teams.
2. Teams share a score track.
3. Teammates may share summits.
4. A summit scores only once per team, regardless of how many teammate goats occupy it.
5. If an opposing player reaches a team-controlled summit, all goats from that team occupying the summit are returned to the base of that mountain.
6. The attacking goat takes control of the summit.
7. All other Mountain Goats rules remain unchanged.
